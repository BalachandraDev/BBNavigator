Patient Registration Module
The Patient Registration page is used by front-desk staff to register a new patient.
Fields: Full name, Date of birth, Gender, Phone number, National ID.
Validations: Full name is mandatory. Date of birth cannot be in the future. Phone number must contain exactly 10 digits. National ID must be unique across all patients.
Business rule: A duplicate patient is blocked when the National ID already exists.
Roles: Front-desk staff can create and edit registrations. Doctors can only view them.
After saving, the user is taken to the Appointment Booking page.Appointment Booking Module
The Appointment Booking page lets front-desk staff book a visit for a registered patient.
Fields: Patient, Department, Doctor, Appointment date, Time slot.
Validations: Appointment date cannot be in the past. A doctor cannot be booked twice for the same time slot. Department is mandatory.
Business rule: Appointments can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the time slot.
Workflow - Outpatient visit: 1. Register patient. 2. Book appointment. 3. Doctor consultation. 4. Generate invoice.Billing Module
The Invoice page is generated after a consultation is completed.
Validations: Discount cannot exceed 20 percent of the invoice total. Payment mode is mandatory.
Business rule: Only users with the Billing Manager role can approve a discount.
Roles: Billing Manager can approve discounts and issue refunds. Front-desk staff can only collect payments.
